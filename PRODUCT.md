# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Published as a Claude Artifact (private link shared with friends). Single self-contained page preferred so it works well on mobile and degrades offline.

## Users
Alberto and friends from Madrid, on their phones during a 4-day trip (Thu 8 – Sun 11 Oct 2026): Madrid → Brussels → Ghent → Bruges → Madrid. Situations: standing at a station checking the next train, deciding where to eat, reading the history of a landmark before entering. Secondary use before the trip: reading background.

## Product Purpose
A personal, Spanish-language trip guide for the Belgium trip: confirmed flights and hotels, day-by-day itinerary, transport (lines, prices, times), history of each landmark with reading links, local food and bars, budget, and pending confirmations. Success: in the street, anyone finds the next step in seconds; at the hotel, it is worth reading.

## Positioning
Not a generic Belgium guide: it is this exact itinerary (real flights, hotels, train times, free-tour meeting point, local-not-tourist food picks) with the author's own tips and anecdotes.

## Operating Context
Mobile, often outdoors, rain likely (10–15°C), possibly poor data. Needs one-tap links to Google Maps and official sites. Dates are fixed; trip starts the day after this build.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Content is provided in full by the user (source brief in the conversation): flights IB0597/IB0602, hotels All In One (Brussels), Ghent River Hotel, B&B Valant (Bruges), transport per leg, itinerary per day, budget €517–627, gastronomy, alternatives, links, practical tips, and a pending-confirmation checklist. Language: Spanish. Open decisions: none recorded. Some prices/times are estimates and must be shown as given, not invented.

## Brand Commitments
None. Author: Alberto. Visual direction to be proposed in the design step.

## Evidence on Hand
All trip data and links in the user's brief. No photos supplied; no testimonials, none to fabricate.

## Product Principles
1. Next action first: the day and the next step are findable in one tap.
2. Mobile in the rain: thumb-reachable, readable outdoors, light on data.
3. Reading and doing are separate: quick logistics up front, history and anecdotes layered below.
4. Show uncertainty honestly: estimates and pending items stay marked as such.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable outdoors (strong contrast), tap targets sized for thumbs, respect reduced motion.
